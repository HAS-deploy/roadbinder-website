RoadBinder

Organize DOT compliance documents and inspection records on your iPhone. Get reminders before things expire.

Coming soon to the App Store
RoadBinder is not an ELD. It is not DOT-certified or DOT-approved. It is not legal advice. RoadBinder organizes documents and reminds you of expirations — use a certified ELD for hours-of-service tracking, and consult a qualified compliance professional for legal questions.

What it does

  • Import compliance PDFs and images, or scan paper with the camera.
  • Tag annual inspections, driver inspections, maintenance records, registration, insurance, permits, licenses, medical cards, drug & alcohol program docs, IFTA, BOLs.
  • Track vehicles, trailers, drivers — link documents to each.
  • Get 90/60/30/7/1-day reminders before expirations.
  • Export an audit-ready PDF packet of selected documents + a summary cover page.

Privacy

Documents and records stay on your device. RoadBinder has no backend; the only outbound traffic is opt-out anonymous product analytics. We never read, upload, or scan document contents.

Pricing

  • Every install gets 7 days of full Pro free. No payment needed during the trial.
  • After the trial: Free tier covers 1 vehicle, 1 driver, and up to 5 documents.
  • RoadBinder Pro: $14.99 / month or $119.99 / year. Removes the limits and unlocks audit-packet export and Face ID lock.

Availability

RoadBinder is currently available in the United States only. It is built for professional truck drivers, owner-operators, and small fleet administrators.
